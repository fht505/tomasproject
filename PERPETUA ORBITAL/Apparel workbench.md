   and were never replaced. Five open slots for new phrases; every new phrase
   goes through the standard screen (Justia discovery → TSDR per serial,
   containing-marks rule) before any art is made.

3. **Niche competition reality** (from launch research): teacher shirts ~870K
   competing listings vs fall candles ~44K — apparel is the crowded end of
   the shop. Batch-3 went all-candles for that reason. Any apparel batch-5
   should chase *narrower* niches or seasonal windows, not more of the same.
4. **Zero sales data yet** (shop-wide) — the niche-focus decision (which
   niche earns batch 5) was always gated on 2–4 weeks of stats. Etsy API is
   down (support ticket pending); views/favorites unavailable until it's back.
5. **Seasonal candidates** for apparel batch-5 (unscreened, idea-stage):

   grandma/teacher gifting window (needs listing by ~mid-Oct), New Year
   "Era" refreshes (2027 datings of B1/B14/B18 format).

## Ground rules (unchanged)
- Every phrase TM-screened before art; printed text is what gets screened.
- Etsy titles: ~15 words, noun-first, no gift-phrases (Aug-2025 guidance);
  13 tags × 20 chars.
- Prices carry the size ladder; margins re-verified against Printify costs
  at stage time, not assumed.
- Operator approval gate before anything publishes.
