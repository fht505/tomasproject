## What we found

The strongest agent-operable lane round 3 found — the only platform whose LEGAL TERMS contain programmatic monetization: Store Publishing Terms (Feb 2026) grant 80% of user fees and PPE "allows you to programmatically charge for events in your Actor source code." Agentic payments (x402/Skyfire) let AI agents discover, run and pay for Actors with no Apify account; eligibility is automatic for PPE Actors. Apify reports ~$1.4M/month paid to third-party developers. Different business than design: the product is working software (scrapers, automations), which the operator side of this project already produces.

## Risks

- The $1.4M/month and "many earn $3k" figures are unaudited first-party marketing; a testimonial on the same page took 15 months to first profit
- Agentic-payments feature is ~1 month old and its rules are visibly shifting — re-verify before building
- Open question: whether the PUBLISHER side (create/price/publish an Actor) is fully API-drivable or gated by KYC/manual review
