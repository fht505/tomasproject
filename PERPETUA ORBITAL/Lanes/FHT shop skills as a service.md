## What we found

The strongest lane found in round 2, and the product already exists. ~/FHT AUTO INC/ holds 20 production shop skills running live since June 2026, including a 27KB fht-mitchell-writeup encoding Mitchell 1 line-type discipline and California BAR Write-It-Right constraints. Domain credibility in a trade is the one input that has not been commoditised — anyone can generate a website; almost nobody selling AI to repair shops has written a repair order that printed on BAR paperwork. Market is ~273,000 US independent shops.

## Risks

- For the first 3-5 customers this is a JOB, not a system — you personally on the phone, you personally doing each shop-facts interview
- It only becomes a system if the same shop-facts template works across shops without you. Test that assumption early and cheaply
- Shops are famously cheap on software; distribution is genuinely hard

## Kill criteria

> [!danger] Decided in advance, on purpose
> Free two-week pilot with five non-competing shops. The whole test is one question: do they paste the output unedited, or rewrite it? If they rewrite it, the domain encoding did not transfer.
