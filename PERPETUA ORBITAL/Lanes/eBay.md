## What we found

Best-founded alternative marketplace. The User Agreement (effective 2026-06-28) binds you to responsibility for 'listing content created using tools offered by eBay or third parties such as translation, image editing, and generative artificial intelligence tools' — contract-level acknowledgment rather than silence. Its automation ban carries 'except with the prior express permission of eBay', and the Developers Program is that permission. Dropshipping from a wholesale supplier is expressly allowed, so Printify fulfilment is compliant.
