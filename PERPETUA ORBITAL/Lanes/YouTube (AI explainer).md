## What we found

Ground truth on the July 2025 change: a RENAME of the long-standing repetitious-content rule to "inauthentic content", not an AI ban. YouTube states in writing that AI-assisted channels remain YPP-eligible if content is original and not "mass-produced, generic, repetitive, or manipulative"; disclosure applies only to REALISTIC synthetic media (AI scripts/ideas/captions explicitly exempt). But YouTube's own examples of banned mass-production (superficially-varied narrated stories, same-narration slideshows) describe the standard faceless-AI format, and verifiers repeatedly flagged that enforcement is materially harsher than the written policy.

## Risks

- The claim that disclosure carries no reach/monetization penalty was REFUTED - do not assume disclosure is cost-free
- A reported Jan 2026 termination wave against mass-produced AI channels failed number-verification but verifiers referenced the event as real
- A 4M-view AI channel proves distribution, not 2026 payout - its growth predates the current enforcement posture
