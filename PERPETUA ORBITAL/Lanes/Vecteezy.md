## What we found

The ONLY platform across both research rounds whose binding contract authorises BOTH AI content and automated publishing. Contributor Agreement s3(e) permits uploading 'using internal Vecteezy tools or other tools approved by Vecteezy (e.g., FTP)', names approved third-party uploaders, and its AI policy accepts AI-rendered images with no release required for text-prompt-only work. Everywhere else the best case was an unwritten gap — and a written yes is not the same asset.

## Risks

- The binding constraint is the weekly Submission Quota, which tightens on low approval rate, where REJECTED FILES STILL CONSUME QUOTA and technically-rejected files can never be resubmitted. A high-volume low-quality pipeline throttles itself into a corner
- The lane itself is shrinking — Shutterstock's content revenue went $789M (2022) to $760M (2024) while its AI training-data line grew 4.5x to $175M

## Kill criteria

> [!danger] Decided in advance, on purpose
> Treat as a cheap bounded experiment, not a business line. Quality gate before upload is the whole game.
