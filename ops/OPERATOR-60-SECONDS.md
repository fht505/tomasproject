# The only things that need your Etsy login

Everything else is done. These four cannot be automated because they live
inside Shop Manager behind your login — I have no Etsy credentials, and
scripting a logged-in session would trip the same bot defense that already
flagged this network's IP once today.

---

## 1. Share your story  (Shop Manager -> Customize your shop)

Paste this:

FondlyMade makes small-batch designs for the people who hold everything
together - teachers, nurses, dog moms, grandmas, and dads of daughters.
Every design starts in our studio, where we sketch, refine, and letter each
piece with the help of modern AI design tools under our creative direction,
then have it printed and shipped in the USA by our production partner.
Candles for cozy fall evenings, shirts that say the quiet part out loud,
mugs for the emotional-support coffee. If it makes you think of someone -
that is the point.

Why it matters: a completed About section is a VERIFIED Etsy search ranking
input, not just decoration.

## 2. Seller photo  (same page)

A casual phone photo of you. I will not generate a face for this - a
fabricated seller photo is exactly the kind of thing that makes a shop look
fake, and it would break the honesty rule the rest of the shop is built on.
Optional. Skip it if you would rather not.

## 3. Marketplace Insights - 6 searches  (Shop Manager -> Marketplace Insights)

You have 15 searches for 30 days. Spend 6 on our niches:

    fall candle
    teacher shirt
    nurse era
    dog mom gift
    grandma era
    emotional support

Screenshot each result. This is REAL Etsy demand data - the exact thing three
research rounds could not obtain - and it decides which niche batch 3 targets
instead of waiting weeks for our own traffic.

## 4. DO NOT click "Turn on Offsite Ads"

Etsy will keep pitching it as "risk-free". At 15% it takes a tee from $8.72
net to $4.42 - below the $5 floor, where the margin guard would start
refusing to stage listings. You opted out on purpose.

---

# The real fix for "you do it": Etsy API access

If you want me operating inside Etsy directly - editing listings, uploading
photos, reading stats - the sanctioned route is an Etsy developer app under
your account (etsy.com/developers). You create it, approve the scopes, hand
me the key the same way you handed me the Printify token. Then everything
above except the seller photo becomes mine.

Without it, anything inside Shop Manager is yours by necessity, not by
choice.
