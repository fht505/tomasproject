# FondlyMade — shop copy to paste into Etsy

## Brand assets (ops/brand/)

- `icon-500.png` — shop icon. Shop Manager → Settings → Info & Appearance → Shop icon.
- `banner-3360x840.png` — big banner. Same page → Banner → Big banner.

Same visual language as the candle labels on purpose: cream stock,
terracotta double ring, candle-flame F. Shop and product read as one hand.

## Shop title (Info & Appearance → Shop title, 55-char limit)

Cozy fall candles & funny gifts for the ones you love

Every block below maps to a Shop Manager field. The About section is a
verified ranking-relevant input (round-4 research); the policies exist because
three independent sellers reported the same refund mechanics: the seller
refunds the buyer in full, Printify refunds the item only — so the policy
must route problems to photos-first replacement, not open-ended returns.

## About section (Shop Manager → Settings → About)

**Shop headline:**
Warm, funny designs for the people you love most

**Story:**
FondlyMade makes small-batch designs for the people who hold everything
together — teachers, nurses, dog moms, grandmas, and dads of daughters. Every
design starts in our studio, where we sketch, refine, and letter each piece
with the help of modern AI design tools under our creative direction, then
have it printed and shipped in the USA by our production partner. Candles for
cozy fall evenings, shirts that say the quiet part out loud, mugs for the
emotional-support coffee. If it makes you think of someone — that is the
point.

## Policies (Shop Manager → Settings → Policy settings)

Etsy's structured policies mostly toggle; where free text is allowed, use:

**Returns & exchanges:** Because every item is printed to order just for you,
we can't accept returns or exchanges for choosing the wrong size or changing
your mind. But if anything arrives damaged, misprinted, or wrong, message us
a photo within 14 days and we'll send a free replacement — no return
shipping, no hassle.

**Message response promise (internal, not a field):** answer every buyer
message the same day — message response rate is a search ranking input.
