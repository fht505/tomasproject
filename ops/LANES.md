## Ranked Top 5

### #1 — Etsy Digital Downloads (printables, planner/tracker sets, SVG bundles, templates)

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Automation % | ~95%. Agents do niche mining, design generation, mockups, SEO titles/tags, listing copy, weekly analytics. Joe: account ownership, ID verification, batch design approval, AI-disclosure fields. |
| Startup cost | ~$5–$34. $0.20/listing; one-time new-shop setup fee $15–29 if opening a second shop; $0 incremental inside the lane-#1 shop. |
| Platform risk | Medium-low. Etsy's June 10, 2025 Creativity Standards explicitly accommodate AI goods: "Designed by" attribution + AI disclosure; must be seller-original design (template flips banned). Our agent-original designs are the compliant category; enforcement targets *undisclosed* AI. |
| Demand evidence | Etsy 86.5M active buyers (Dec 2025). Semrush US/mo: printable wall art 1,600 + printable art 3,600; wedding invitation template 4,400; habit tracker printable 2,400; free svg files 2,400; chore chart printable 1,300; budget planner printable 390 — plus Etsy-internal search on top. ($3.2B digital-segment estimate: UNVERIFIED, directional only.) |
| Time-to-first-dollar | ~1–4 weeks per community first-sale threads. Payout callout: new-seller payment reserves can hold ~30% of funds 45 days (policy revised Oct 9, 2025) — sale signal fast, cash slower. |

**Agent operation:** mine Semrush + Etsy autocomplete for long-tails with weak
competition, generate original printables/SVGs/templates, 6–10 mockups per
listing, keyword-matrixed listings with disclosure line, batch approval.
Winning fall/holiday designs from the candle lane get repurposed into
wall-art printables and SVG bundles at zero marginal design cost. Weekly:
read shop stats, kill dead listings, double down on favorites.

### #2 — Amazon KDP: niche coloring/activity books + workbooks

| Criterion | Assessment |

**Agent operation:** find underserved coloring/activity themes (identity ×
hobby crossovers, mirroring the low-KD apparel insight), generate genuinely
good interiors (consistent line weight, tested puzzles — not blank-page
spam), covers, metadata. Joe approves, ticks disclosure, publishes ≤3/day at
$9.99+. BSR feedback within 30 days → serialize winners.

## #1 Pick: First-Experiment (Etsy Digital Downloads)

**Week 1:** second shop (clean metrics; $15–29 + ID) or section in shop #1
($0). **25 listings, 3 data-picked micro-niches:** (a) fall/seasonal
printable wall-art sets (reuses candle design language, same Q3–Q4 ramp),
(b) planner/tracker printables (weak template-flip competition our original
work legally outclasses), (c) Halloween/fall SVG bundles (spike begins
~August — ideal timing). Per listing: original asset pack, 6–10 mockups,
long-tail title + 13 tags, disclosure line, instant-download files. Cost
ceiling ~$34. Joe time ~2h.

**Day-30 go/no-go:** GO (scale to 100) = ≥1 sale OR ≥150 visits with ≥3%
favorite rate. PIVOT (one retry) = 0 sales but ≥200 visits → rework
thumbnails/pricing. KILL = <200 visits → niche selection failed; rerun
mining before spending another listing dollar.

## Watchlist (revisit on trigger)

- **Adobe Stock AI** — best stock policy (labeled genAI accepted, 33%
  royalty) but 1–3 month moderation queues break the 30-day rule. Trigger:
  reviews back under ~2 weeks.
- **Freepik AI contributor** — accepts AI with labeling; ~$0.04–0.07/download;
  ~2-month payment lag. Bolt on later from the same asset library.
- **Chrome extension / micro-SaaS** — $5 fee, agents can build it, but
  review + discovery + own payment rail make <30-day first-dollar a coin
  flip. Lane #5–6 material.

## Do Not Bother (specifically disqualified)

1. **Faceless YouTube/Shorts** — YPP thresholds block <30-day monetization;
   July 15, 2025 "inauthentic content" policy demonetizes mass AI content.
2. **Stock music** — AI bans across Envato/AudioJungle, Pond5, Artlist.
3. **Shutterstock** — no external AI submissions accepted at all.
4. **Canva Creators** — AI-heavy content rejected; element applications closed.
5. **Redbubble/TeePublic** — 50% platform fee on Standard accounts (Sept 1,
   2025) on a saturated marketplace; margins negligible.
6. **3D model marketplaces** — default "No AI" filters (Cults3D), real-print
   photo requirements (MakerWorld) we can't meet without hardware.
7. **GPT Store** — US-only engagement pilot, no general payment rail.

## Recommended queue

Lane #2 = **Etsy digital downloads** (launch now; seasonal window + shared
pipeline). Lane #3 = **KDP coloring/activity books** (start the clock;
accept 60-day royalty lag). Lane #4 = **Notion templates** (join waitlist
immediately; Gumroad + free-gallery funnel meanwhile). Fiverr = fast-cash +
market intel when operator wants it. Creative Fabrica = zero-cost
syndication of everything.
