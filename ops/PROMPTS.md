# BATCH-01 — ChatGPT Image Prompts (copy-paste)

You can start these from your phone in the ChatGPT app. One design at a
time: paste the prompt, regenerate until it's right, then **Save Image**.

## Rules for every design

1. **CHECK SPELLING LETTER BY LETTER.** AI text renders can silently
   misspell. A misspelled print = wasted listing. If any letter is off,
   regenerate or ask "fix the spelling of X, keep everything else".
2. Save with the **exact filename code** (A1.png, B7.png …) — my pipeline
   matches files to listings by that code.
3. Apparel (B-series): design must be **isolated on a transparent or plain
   white background — no shirt, no person, no mockup**. If ChatGPT draws it
   on a shirt, reply: "give me only the print design itself on a transparent
   background, no shirt, no mockup."
4. Candle labels (A-series): a flat rectangular label design, full-bleed
   cream background is correct (the label itself is the artwork).
5. Taste check: would a stranger pay $24 for it? If it looks clip-arty,
   regenerate with "cleaner, more premium, less clutter".
6. When done, get the PNGs to me (upload here, or drop in Drive/repo) and
   I take it from there — upscaling, mockups, drafts, listings.

Iteration phrases that work: "make the text 30% larger" · "fewer decorative
elements" · "more distressed vintage texture" · "two ink colors only" ·
"increase contrast for printing".
