# Batch 4 — Thanksgiving/fall table printables (digital downloads)

Drafted 2026-08-04 from measured competition. The shape won on data: 19 of 20
scouted phrases sit under 2,000 competing listings, against 3.16M for
"printable wall art" and 770K for "digital planner". Specificity is the whole
game, same as the candles.

Products are DIGITAL DOWNLOADS in the existing KindlyPut shop: PDF, printed by
the buyer. No production partner, no shipping, no reserve exposure, ~100%
margin after Etsy's $0.20 listing fee. Seasonal window: buyers shop
Thanksgiving printables from late September; live well before then.

## The six concepts, with their EXACT printed wording

The trademark screen for printables works differently than candles: the search
phrase is usually a descriptive product term nobody can register ("thanksgiving
table numbers"), but the words PRINTED ON the artwork can collide with
registered marks in Class 16 (printed matter/stationery). So each concept below
separates the two. Only the printed wording needs screening.

### P1 — Thanksgiving conversation cards (22–299 competitors)
Set of 24 cards. Printed wording, verbatim:
- Header card: "What are you thankful for?"
- Card prompts, e.g.: "What made you laugh hardest this year?", "Who at this
  table are you grateful for, and why?", "What smell means fall to you?",
  "What's the best thing you ate this year?" (20 more, full list at design time)
- SCREEN: "What are you thankful for" (phrase as heading). Prompts are
  sentences — no realistic mark risk, spot-check only.

### P2 — Place cards, "gather" theme (110–230 competitors)
Printable place cards, blank name line. Printed wording:
- "gather" (lowercase serif, decorative) on one variant
- "thankful" / "grateful" on two more variants
- SCREEN CAREFULLY: "GATHER", "THANKFUL", "GRATEFUL" as standalone words on
  paper goods. GATHER especially — short brandable word, home-decor adjacent,
  exactly the shape that gets registered in Class 16.
- Fallback if screening kills the standalone words: leaf motifs + name line
  only, zero text. The product survives without the words.

### P3 — Thanksgiving menu template (123–1,350 competitors)
Editable/printable dinner menu. Printed wording:
- "Thanksgiving Menu" or "Turkey Day" header + course labels ("Starters",
  "Mains", "Pies")
- SCREEN: "TURKEY DAY" (Class 16). "Thanksgiving Menu" is purely descriptive.

### P4 — Table numbers 1–12 (170 competitors)
Numerals with autumn line-art (wheat, oak leaf, pumpkin per number).
- Printed wording: numerals only, optional word "Table".
- SCREEN: nothing. Zero text risk — this is the safest product in the set.

### P5 — Pumpkin pie recipe card (70 competitors)
Decorative printable recipe card, classic pie recipe, apothecary-label style
(reuses the candle-label design language).
- Printed wording: "Pumpkin Pie", ingredient/step text.
- SCREEN: "PUMPKIN PIE" standalone (descriptive, expect clear); recipe text is
  functional, no mark risk. Recipe itself written by us — recipes as such are
  not copyrightable, but the TEXT must be original, not copied.

### P6 — Gratitude jar kit (599–668 competitors)
Printable jar label + 30 small prompt slips + instruction card.
- Printed wording: "Gratitude Jar" label; slip prompts are sentences.
- SCREEN: "GRATITUDE JAR" (Class 16). Generic-descriptive, expect clear, but
  it is the product's headline so it gets verified, not assumed.

## What screening covers (and the classes)

Goods: printed matter / paper goods / stationery — **Class 16**, plus
downloadable digital files — **Class 9**. Etsy shop-name sweep same as always
(a shop BRANDED "Gather" selling paper goods would block P2's word even if no
federal mark exists).

Phrases to screen, complete list:
1. "What are you thankful for" (P1 heading)
2. GATHER (P2, standalone decorative word — highest risk in the set)
3. THANKFUL (P2)
4. GRATEFUL (P2)
5. TURKEY DAY (P3)
6. PUMPKIN PIE (P5, expect trivially clear)
7. GRATITUDE JAR (P6)

Seven screens for six products. Everything else printed is sentences,
numerals, or course labels — descriptive text with no mark exposure.

## Format decisions (locked unless operator objects)

- PDF, US Letter + A4, 300dpi, print-at-home friendly (no full-bleed
  dependence). P3 menu additionally as editable via Canva template link ONLY
  if that can be done without a Canva Pro requirement for buyers — otherwise
  PDF-only, sold as "print and hand-write".
- Listing images: styled flat-lay mockups of the printed pieces — these sell
  the product; bare PDFs do not.
- 5 files max per Etsy digital listing; bundles zip.
- Descriptions state clearly: digital download, nothing shipped, personal use
  license, no refunds per policy (photos-first policy does not apply to
  digital).

## Explicitly NOT in batch 4

- Halloween SVGs (fall gnome, pumpkin bundles): viable numbers but a different
  buyer (crafters with cutting machines) and a different file discipline
  (layered SVG/DXF). Batch 5 candidate if table printables convert.
- Anything editable-template-flip adjacent: research says template flips are
  banned; everything here is original design.
