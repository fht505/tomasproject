## Why the pilot is #4, not #1

Brake topics are 4 of the 16 slots (#4, #6, #10, #15). Piloting "shake when
you brake" means the rotor/caliper/pad diagram templates built for the pilot
get reused three more times — maximum template leverage per the
build-once-reuse-everywhere pipeline design. White smoke (#1) needs
engine-internals diagrams no other video shares.
